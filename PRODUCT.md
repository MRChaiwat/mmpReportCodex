# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Primary users are admins, managers, sales leads, and sales representatives who need to review budget and actual sales performance from uploaded Excel reports. Future users include executives, product managers, and finance teams who need higher-level performance, variance, product mix, and data quality views.

## Product Purpose

This product turns large sales budget Excel files into an authenticated online dashboard. It helps users compare Budget 26 against Actual 25/24 and Budget 25, track sales performance, inspect area and product-group contribution, and surface suspicious or incomplete data before decisions are made.

## Brand Personality

Serious, clear, trustworthy. The product should feel analytical and operational, closer to a simplified Power BI experience than a marketing site.

## Anti-references

Avoid loud colors, decorative dashboards, card-heavy marketing layouts, landing-page composition, glossy SaaS hero patterns, and playful visual treatments that reduce trust in the numbers.

## Design Principles

1. Make the key variance obvious before showing detail.
2. Prioritize scan speed for managers and executives.
3. Keep upload and data freshness status explicit.
4. Treat data quality warnings as first-class decision context.
5. Keep role-based access visible and predictable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Use restrained contrast-safe colors, avoid relying on color alone for variance or warning states, support Thai and English text without clipping, and keep motion minimal and state-driven.
